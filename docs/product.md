# Product

## What VECAI is

VECAI is a project management workspace for construction companies in
Kenya. One workspace holds every project site, the tasks on each site,
material stock, contracts and drawings, daily site reports, and the budget
ledger.

## Who it is for

- Project managers who need a live view of every site.
- Site engineers and site managers logging progress from the field.
- Quantity surveyors tracking BOQs and cost variance.
- Architects distributing the right drawing revisions.
- Project owners checking progress without phone calls.

## Problems it solves

- Daily progress lived in paper diaries and WhatsApp threads — nothing
  was searchable or comparable across sites.
- Material stock was counted by hand, so shortages were discovered when
  a pour or installation had to stop.
- Drawings and contracts were scattered across shared drives with no
  version discipline.
- Budget vs. actual was worked out in spreadsheets weeks after the fact.

## How it works

1. An admin creates projects and invites the team.
2. Project managers plan tasks, budgets and milestones per project.
3. Site teams log daily updates, material deliveries and issues.
4. Documents and reports live next to the project they belong to.
5. The intelligence page answers plain-language questions computed from
   the live data (e.g. "weekly status report", "budget variance").

## Roles

- **admin** — manage projects, organisation settings, team invites.
- **member** — day-to-day work: tasks, materials, documents, site updates.
- **viewer** — read-only access to every page.
